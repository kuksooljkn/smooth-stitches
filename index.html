Frequently asked questions

Before you book

Can I get a quote from a photo?

A photo can help Sara provide an initial estimate. Final pricing may change after the garment and construction are inspected.

Do you provide mobile fittings?

Mobile service may be available depending on your location, the garment, and the schedule. Include your general area when requesting a quote.

How quickly can an alteration be completed?

Turnaround depends on the garment, complexity, current workload, and your deadline. Sara will give you a realistic timeframe before accepting the job.

What should I include in my email?

Include the item, requested alteration, deadline, general Houston-area location, and clear photos of the garment.
